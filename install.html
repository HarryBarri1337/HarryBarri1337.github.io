Install SkinQuest

Add SkinQuest to your home screen for a full-screen experience, quick access, and a reliable offline app shell.

iOS

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open skinquestcs.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
A

Android

  1. Open SkinQuest in Chrome.
  2. Tap the browser menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation.
PC

Desktop

  1. Open SkinQuest in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm Install.

Open on your phone

Scan the QR code with your camera, then follow the steps for your device.

Updates happen automatically

SkinQuest checks the network first and updates cached app files in the background. When you are offline, the previously cached public app shell remains available.
