Terms of Service

These terms explain the basic rules for using SkinQuest during the current reward-platform setup.

1. What SkinQuest is

SkinQuest lets users earn coins through free partner tasks and redeem fixed CS2 rewards when stock is available. SkinQuest does not accept deposits, sell coins, run cases, run roulette, or offer randomized gambling rewards.

2. Eligibility and account rules

You must meet the minimum age required in your country and by each provider, and have guardian permission where required. Use one account only and keep access secure. Do not use bots, automation, VPN or location abuse, duplicate identities, or dishonest survey answers. Material violations may lead to coin reversal, reward rejection, suspension, or deletion.

3. Offerwall completions

Coins are credited only after a partner provider confirms a task. Some offers may be delayed, rejected, limited by country or device, or reversed by the provider. SkinQuest cannot guarantee that every attempted offer will credit.

4. Rewards and stock

Rewards have fixed coin prices and limited stock. A request reserves stock and deducts coins, but remains subject to manual review. If SkinQuest rejects or refunds a request before delivery, the deducted coins are returned once. Returned coins count toward XP in the same way as earned coins. After a valid Steam trade is sent or accepted, a reward is normally final unless mandatory law requires otherwise.

5. Steam trade links

You must provide a valid Steam trade URL to receive approved rewards. SkinQuest only needs your public trade URL. Never share your Steam password, Steam Guard code, Steam API key, or login credentials.

6. Availability and changes

Offers, coin values, stock, and features can change or be unavailable. SkinQuest may correct obvious pricing or stock errors before delivery and will refund deducted coins if a request cannot be fulfilled. We do not guarantee uninterrupted access, a specific survey, or a specific processing time.

To the extent allowed by law, SkinQuest is not responsible for provider decisions, Steam restrictions, indirect loss, or events outside reasonable control. Nothing here removes mandatory consumer rights.

8. Suspension, termination, and disputes

We may restrict an account while suspected fraud or abuse is reviewed. You may delete your account after pending reward requests are resolved. Swedish law governs these terms where permitted; mandatory protections in your country continue to apply.

9. Contact

For support questions, reward issues, or account help, contact support@skinquestcs.com.
