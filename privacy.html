Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SkinQuest processes personal data for accounts, surveys, rewards, support, security, and analytics.

1. Controller and contact

SkinQuest is the data controller for data processed directly by this service. Contact support@skinquestcs.com for privacy questions or rights requests.

2. Data we process

We process account email and ID, authentication records, coin balance and history, level progress, survey event identifiers, reward requests, Steam ID and public trade URL, notification choices, support messages, security records, IP-derived rate-limit hashes, browser language, user agent, and page URL.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Account, coin, survey, reward, and delivery data is processed to perform our service contract. Fraud prevention, security, support, and service improvement rely on our legitimate interests. Optional analytics or marketing technologies rely on consent where required. Legal obligations may also require processing.

4. Providers and recipients

Supabase provides authentication and database hosting; CPX Research and any activated survey partners handle offers and verification; Resend handles transactional email; Google Analytics 4 measures site usage when enabled. Administrators can access only the data needed to review support, fraud, coins, and reward delivery. Each external provider also applies its own terms and privacy policy.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Sweden or the EEA. Where required, transfers should rely on an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or another lawful safeguard. Provider policies contain their specific locations and safeguards.

6. Retention

Active account and transaction records are kept while the account is in use. Support records are normally deleted within 24 months after closure; security and rate-limit records within 12 months; analytics follows the configured GA4 retention period. Reward and accounting records may be kept longer when needed for disputes, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Deletion removes or anonymizes data unless retention is legally required.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Use Settings to delete the account or contact support for a data copy. We may need to verify your identity.

8. Complaints and children

You may complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or your local supervisory authority. SkinQuest is not intended for anyone below the minimum digital-consent age in their country. Survey providers may impose higher age limits; users under 18 need guardian permission where required.

9. Steam safety

SkinQuest only needs your public trade URL and, when connected, public Steam identity. Never provide a Steam password, Steam Guard code, or API key. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.
